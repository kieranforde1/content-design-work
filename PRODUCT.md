# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Hiring managers and senior content designers at tech companies, reviewing a portfolio. Time-poor, pattern-matching for seniority signals. They are skimming before they read. They arrive expecting to see shallow AI-generated portfolios and need to be convinced in 10 seconds that this is different.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for Kieran Forde, Senior Content Designer. The site demonstrates ten years of product content, content systems, and team leadership across a national statistics institute (ONS), a product startup (Edo/Trim), and HubSpot. Success means a hiring manager clicks through to a case study. The work proves the seniority; the portfolio's design reinforces the message without competing with it.

## Brand Personality

Confident, considered, understated. Senior without being corporate. The work speaks; the chrome steps back. Not a template. Not an AI portfolio. A person with taste who knows what matters.

## Anti-references

- Purple gradients, glassmorphism, gradient text, "modern SaaS" dashboard aesthetics
- AI-generated card grids (icon + heading + body, repeated ad nauseam)
- Inter everywhere (signifier of zero typographic judgment)
- Nested card-on-card layouts
- Anything that looks like a template or a Framer site
- Hero-metric blocks: big number, small label, gradient accent (SaaS cliche)
- Side-stripe borders on cards (>1px left/right colored accent)

## Design Principles

1. **The work is the proof.** Outcomes and stats earn their place because they're real. Don't dress them up; let them land.
2. **Seniority is shown, not stated.** Structural decisions, strategic framing, and restrained chrome signal level better than job-title copy.
3. **Chrome steps back.** Navigation, footer, section labels, tag system — all deferential. Nothing decorative that doesn't also inform.
4. **Skimmable first, readable second.** Every card should communicate its signal in a glance. The summary rewards a second pass.
5. **Practise what you preach.** A content designer's portfolio is itself a content design artefact. Every label, heading, and summary is the work.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Sufficient colour contrast on all text. No motion-dependent information. Semantic heading hierarchy throughout.
