## Context: startups didn't know there was a free option

HubSpot's startup discount programme had a pricing page and signup flow that obscured the free option — users were seeing discounted paid plans without realising they could start for free. This was losing potential customers who weren't ready to pay, and creating confusion among those who were.

## The work: redesigned flow, clearer options, post-signup upsells

Redesigned the pricing page to surface the free option clearly alongside discounted paid plans. Added post-signup upsell moments for users who came in free but showed buying signals. Rewrote the flow copy to make the value proposition of each tier clear.

## Outcomes: ~$200k in new annual deal revenue

Average increase of $16,450 in first-time purchases per month — ~$200k in new annual deal revenue.
