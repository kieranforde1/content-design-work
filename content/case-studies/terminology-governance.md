## Discovery: understanding why term documentation wasn't happening

Product teams knew their terminology was inconsistent but weren't documenting it. Discovery research to understand the blockers — finding that the existing process was too slow, too reliant on content designers, and produced outputs teams didn't use.

## Workshop design: a self-serve toolkit teams could run without a content designer

Designed a self-serve workshop toolkit that product teams could run independently — a 60-minute session structure with facilitation guides and templates. Pilot sessions identified 27 terms in a single session.

## Scaling and buy-in: leadership buy-in and a 2025 rollout plan

501 issues resolved by teams acting on workshop outputs alone — 13.2% of all issues identified, with no automation. 22 targeted quality sprint reports sent to product owners across three product groups. Leadership buy-in secured for a 2025 rollout.
