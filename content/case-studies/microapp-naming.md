## Context: four microapps, each needing a distinct identity

Four new microapps were being built — Campaign Assistant, Guide Creator, Blog Ideas Generator, and Clip Creator. Each needed a name and a content identity that would work both inside HubSpot and in external marketing.

## Campaign Assistant: the lead product announcement at INBOUND 2023

Content design for Campaign Assistant — the lead announcement at INBOUND 2023. The product launched to 3,000+ signups in its first three months.

## Guide Creator naming: a data-driven naming process Marketing has since adopted

The naming process developed for Guide Creator — testing candidate names against user comprehension, recall, and alignment with HubSpot's brand — was adopted by Marketing as their standard approach for product naming. The 8.56% increase in WordPress plugin signup conversion came from content work on the plugin's onboarding flow alongside the naming project.
