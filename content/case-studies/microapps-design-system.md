## Context: microapps were acquiring users, but not converting them

HubSpot's microapps (free tools like the Email Signature Generator) were bringing in large volumes of traffic and users — but those users weren't converting into HubSpot free or paid customers at the rate they could. The "product bridge" experience — the moment when a microapp user is invited into HubSpot — was inconsistent and underperforming.

## The work: principles, components, and content patterns for bridge experiences

Built a design system for the microapp-to-HubSpot bridge experience — defining content principles, component guidance, and patterns for the moments of handoff. The system gave product teams a shared framework for designing bridge experiences consistently and at speed.

## Outcomes: product-bridged signups grew from 7.3% to 21.5%

Product-bridged signups grew from 7.3% in July 2024 to 21.5% by December — a 14.2 percentage point increase over 5 months.
