## Context: viral links had no consistent format or approach

HubSpot's viral links — the links that users share with colleagues to invite them to HubSpot products and features — had no consistent structure. Some explained what they were sharing. Some didn't. The language varied widely.

## The work: audit, pattern, experiment

Audited the existing viral link landscape across HubSpot. Developed a consistent pattern: every link explains the feature, the benefit, and the cost (free or otherwise). Ran three live experiments testing the new pattern against the existing links.

## Outcomes: 42,000 more signups initiated

42,000 more signups initiated across three experiments. 500 more signups completed.
