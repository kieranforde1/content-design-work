## Context: new users were leaving without activating key tools

New HubSpot users were landing in Forms and Deals but leaving without activating — the empty state gave them no clear starting point or reason to engage with the feature.

## The work: content design for empty states and guided tours

Designed empty states with clear value props and entry points, and guided tours that walked users through the key steps to activation. The content was designed to be low-friction — short, specific, and action-oriented.

## Scale: rolled out to Reporting — and performed even better

When the guided tour pattern was rolled out to Reporting, completion hit 78% and customers using the guided tour increased by 22 percentage points.
