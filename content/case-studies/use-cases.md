## Context: use cases were agreed. The language for them wasn't

HubSpot's growth team had aligned on the use cases to feature across Free and Starter — the key tasks and value propositions to surface to new users. But the language for those use cases was inconsistent across surfaces. Different designers were writing their own versions of the same concept.

## The work: a framework, not just a copy doc

Built a content framework that enabled product designers to frame HubSpot's most valuable use cases in consistent, customer-facing language. The framework defined the vocabulary, structure, and principles — and 120 new copy snippets went into the shared content library (5× more than any other contributor).

## Outcomes: measurable impact on conversion

209% increase in touchless purchase CVR. 9,000 net additions from improved Free–Starter conversion rate.
