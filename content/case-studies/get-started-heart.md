## Context: a signup experience that didn't reflect what HubSpot could do for people

HubSpot's signup flow had accumulated decisions made for technical or operational reasons that didn't serve users well. The experience didn't convey HubSpot's value, and new users were arriving in the product without a clear sense of what to do next.

## The work: vision, principles, research, and iteration

Co-led content design for a multi-quarter signup redesign. Defined the content vision and principles — a shared reference point for decision-making throughout the project — and worked through multiple rounds of research and iteration across the signup and onboarding flow.

## Outcomes: early experiments showed 25% activation uplift

Early experiments on the redesigned flow showed a 25% increase in activation and a 52% increase in monetisation.
