## Context: the most visited microapp wasn't driving signups

HubSpot's Email Signature Generator gets more pageviews than any other microapp — 4× the next most visited. But it wasn't converting those visitors into signups. Monthly signups were 468 — low relative to traffic volume.

## The experiment: a modal at the moment of highest intent

Added sign-up CTAs after the email signature copy step — the moment when a user has just completed their task and is most likely to be receptive to what comes next. The modal surfaced the signup prompt at peak intent rather than interrupting the flow.

## Outcomes: monthly signups grew from 468 to 6,452

A 13.8× increase in monthly signups.
