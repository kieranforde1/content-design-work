## Context: a new product area with no established content patterns

HubSpot's AI Agents team was building Agent Builder and Custom Assistant — new products with no existing content patterns, terminology, or IA to draw from.

## The work: concept mapping, naming systems, IA, and a content workflow

Content design lead for Agent Builder and Breeze Studio. Work included concept mapping to establish shared vocabulary across the team, naming systems for agents and tools, IA decisions for the product surface, and a content workflow to scale across the team.

The 85% findability rate for the AI nav label came from a naming research sprint — testing candidate labels before committing to one.
