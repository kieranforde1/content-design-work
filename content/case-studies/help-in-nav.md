## Context: Free and Starter users were raising support tickets instead of finding answers themselves

Free and Starter HubSpot customers who needed help were defaulting to raising support tickets — even for questions that could be answered through documentation or in-app guidance. This was expensive for HubSpot and slower for users.

## The work: information architecture and step-by-step content snippets

Redesigned the help UI for Free and Starter users. Work included IA restructuring so the right help surfaced for the right context, and writing step-by-step content snippets that gave users actionable guidance without leaving the product.

## Outcomes: more self-serve, fewer tickets

Self-serve support increased by 22.96%. Support tickets from Starter customers decreased by 26.65 percentage points.
