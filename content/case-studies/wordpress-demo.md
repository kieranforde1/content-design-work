## Context: a fictional narrative that got in the way of the demo's job

HubSpot's WordPress plugin demo used a fictional brand narrative — a constructed story about a fictional company — to walk users through the product. The narrative added length and cognitive load without adding clarity. Users were completing the demo less often than they should, and signing up at a lower rate than traffic warranted.

## The work: feature-focused, ordered by value, easy to update

Replaced the fictional narrative with feature-focused demo content ordered by value — leading with the most useful and differentiating capabilities. Rewrote to be direct and specific. Also restructured the demo so future feature changes could be rolled in without rewriting the whole thing.

## Outcomes: 12.9% more demo completions; productised as the new standard

12.9% more demo completions. Users who completed the demo were 24.3% more likely to sign up. The new approach was productised as the standard format for the WordPress plugin demo.
