## Voice of the customer: terminology inconsistency is a named, documented customer pain

270,000 terminology-related issues found in automated scans of live product strings. 34,000 critical issues visible in production. 86% of all automated writing suggestions flagged to designers are terminology issues — this is the dominant content quality problem in HubSpot's product.

## The work: three strands, one goal

A multi-strand project to make terminology consistent at scale:

1. **Governance and workshops** (Strand 1) — a self-serve workshop toolkit for product teams to identify, document, and own their terminology
2. **Terminology Checker** (Strand 2) — an AI-assisted proof of concept for automated terminology checking
3. **Engineering rebuild** (Strand 3) — translating the prototype into a rules-based checker that runs in the code review workflow

Each strand has its own case study page. See [terminology-governance.html], [terminology-checker.html], and [terminology-engineering.html].
