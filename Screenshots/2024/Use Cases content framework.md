# Use Cases content framework

[*Check out the Loom introduction*](https://www.loom.com/share/70bcd37a284f41cc9e5579b1220d490e) *for a quick explanation of how to use this framework.*

“Driving usage” is one of our “Strategic Six” P0 objectives for HubSpot in 2024\. We’re focusing on usage because product data shows that when customers use HubSpot more, [they grow faster](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1MeGyOoSBKxjPpv6q5_e0EZ6vd22fc8cAxiuNJjUvl50/edit#slide=id.g29be02d0ae5_0_4845) and [they are less likely to downgrade](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1MeGyOoSBKxjPpv6q5_e0EZ6vd22fc8cAxiuNJjUvl50/edit#slide=id.g29be02d0ae5_0_4861).

This content framework for use cases will: 

* Help us frame our use cases in ways that resonate with Business Builders \- our target audience for HubSpot’s Free and Starter plans  
* Enable us to talk about these concepts consistently across the user journey  
* Give teams flexibility to judge how and when to adapt content  
* Make sure that we’re using language and value propositions that are aligned across marketing, growth and the rest of the product

# **What are use cases?**

Use cases capture the core value propositions of each hub. They help us explain and measure the specific ways that HubSpot can solve for our customers. Without use cases, it'd be more difficult to understand users’ goals and how they are using HubSpot.

Use cases have been agreed and defined at the “federal” level \- they apply across product lines and teams. This framework will adapt our language around use cases to frame them in an intuitive way for [Business Builders](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/188fEbmEMbYnVKPKmj1IxNzd7wiyF6ydPBMoy461TrRU/edit#slide=id.g1cf4af9997d_0_956): the target audience for HubSpot’s Free and Starter plans, and the main focus of the Growth Product Line.

# **Use case positioning**

Individual use cases are a great way to create a specific connection with Business Builders. At the same time, keep in mind that one of HubSpot’s greatest assets is that it’s an all-in-one platform. We need to strike a balance between being specific and keeping focus on our broader offer.

Often, the best approach is to combine use cases. Show how use cases are connected through HubSpot's Smart CRM, and powered by HubSpot AI to help Business Builders grow.

For more information about positioning for Business Builders, check out the [Starter Customer Platform Relaunch Narrative](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1qz4VTRhEh1RVYQ1hkbE5iewnvvNxiQT_Z-eSRNQZm9k/edit#slide=id.g2caa48c3953_0_0).

# **Use case value propositions** {#use-case-value-propositions}

For each use case, value proposition copy snippets can be used to expand on how users can achieve their goals. Value propositions connect the benefits of the use case with the ways users can achieve them.

There's more than one snippet for each use case, and there are different snippets aimed at Free and Starter users.

Snippets aimed at Free users focus on the core value proposition, whereas snippets for Starter customers focus on the benefits that can be unlocked by upgrading to a paid Starter account. 

Use one, some or all of the snippets based on what you think is most relevant to the user at that point in the journey. Remember that most people come to HubSpot with multiple use cases in mind \- aim to make connections for the user and show the value of an all-in-one platform.

Where possible, the snippets are listed in priority order, based on our understanding of what is most important to Business Builders.

# **How to update use cases content**

As we learn more about Business Builders and how they find value from our use cases, we should review and update use case language to reflect what they need. 

To change a content snippet:

- [ ]  Draft your new content  
      - [ ] Check whether the wording repeats or could cause confusion with other snippets, including the [value proposition and benefit statements](#use-case-value-propositions)  
      - [ ] Check the wording doesn’t conflict with other content documentation, such as [\[Free and Starter Relaunch\] Prospect and Customer Messaging Framework](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1A8VmnUAzclIOeEbFtV9IZpmtg-n42vK7oHHfcsa4Q3A/edit#slide=id.g2ca803b3647_5_0)  
      - [ ] Get feedback from other product designers at a [Growth Design Jam](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1lWTVrwHlx-MNdFoTldfFuNMc_ALdhxPad1kiNKU_xtw/edit#slide=id.g2b9798b4a60_0_196)  
- [ ] Update all references to the snippet in this document, including the design rationale and guidance to document the evidence that informed this change (remove the rationale for snippets that have been replaced)  
- [ ] Update the relevant snippets in [Starter Relaunch | Use Cases & Positioning | Atlas](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1xx9G7YIUfjLAqRSRAlViU0vgZzPxtDA9bNZUV53jSo4/edit?pli=1#slide=id.g2cf4afde0ae_1_16)

- [ ] Update the relevant snippets in Writer \- all existing snippets are documented in this template [✍️ Use case framework snippets for Writer](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1Lr1-LMAJwr4q7Lp-rY1wcEXajYA2Xbxtt9gP1Qj_vDY/edit#gid=1595089318), pay attention to the relevant tags so that others can find your snippets easily and consistently

- [ ] Review the end-to-end user journeys that relate to your snippet (including signup, onboarding, playbooks, product tours) to check for copy that needs to be updated \- log any changes with the relevant teams

- [ ] Share your changes in \#ux-team and \#growth product-line
