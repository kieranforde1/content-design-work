Background / Problem:
See: [Framing Doc]  Onboarding from Empty States 

Starter Customers are not getting the onboarding they need to quickly get set up for success. Specifically, when a new Free User / Starter Customer leaves our linear post-signup/purchase onboarding flows, they do not receive enough guidance to help them:


Understand the value of a new tool - how it will solve for their business need / use case
Feel reassured that help is available to them the first time they explore/setup a new tool

In Q1 we ran a proof of concept experiment which included Version 1 of ‘Empty States’ for Marketing Hub tools. This experiment will test Version 2 of Empty States, specifically focusing on the Forms tool.

Objective:

Our business goal is to increase initial Content Activation (Forms) for Marketing Hub Free & Starter customers. 


Our customer goal is to help customers immediately understand how Forms can solve for their business needs and reassure them they have guidance the first time they set the tool up. 

Long term, our goal is to build a Library of Empty States that can be used by teams across the Product Pillar to help drive new-user onboarding/activation.

Hypotheses: 
We believe HubSpot’s Free/Starter tools meet the immediate needs of Business Builders, but new users/customers fail to adopt them because they don’t understand how a tool will solve their business’ needs. 
We also believe the lack of guidance demotivates users/customers as they believe the tool to be too complex and time consuming. 
We believe these pain points are most acute when a user/customer is exploring a tool for the first time, outside our linear post-signup/purchase onboarding flows / centralized User Guides. 
We believe this because: 
The majority of businesses using Free/Starter are early in their business/digitizing journey, at this stage they are seeking to adopt tools that address their immediate needs - but their limited experience might mean they don’t know what these tools are/should be [Source]
Free/Starter customers are experiencing pain around learning our software and do not feel onboarding sets them up effectively which is driving negative trends in customer sentiment and loyalty. [Source]
Software functionality, understanding if/how a tool solves for a need, is paramount to Free/Starter success - relational survey feedback calls this out as a gap/top pain point [Source]
Today, once a user leaves our post-signup/purchase onboarding flows they are largely left alone - product-led onboarding is leveraged inconsistently across HS platform of tools.
